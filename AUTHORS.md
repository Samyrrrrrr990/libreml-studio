# Authors and contributors

LibreML Studio was founded by **Samyar Shafiee** and is developed with contributions from the LibreML Studio community.

The canonical contributor history is the Git commit history. A release may also publish a generated contributor list, subject to contributor preferences and `.mailmap` normalization.

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Licensing a contribution under the community license does not, by itself, necessarily give the project the rights needed to offer that contribution under separate proprietary terms. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

Please do not add automation tools or AI systems as human authors. Contributors remain responsible for reviewing submitted work and disclosing material tool assistance when required by the contribution or research context.
